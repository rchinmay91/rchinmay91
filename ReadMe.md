# 💫 About Me:
I am a versatile Data Scientist and Analytics Engineer who builds end-to-end data solutions, transforming raw enterprise databases into advanced business intelligence frameworks and production-ready AI applications. My expertise spans relational Star Schema modeling, complex SQL data mining, and predictive machine learning architectures alongside context-aware local RAG deployment, fully backed by a rigorous engineering mathematical background in Python, R, SQL, and SAS. I thrive on translating chaotic, multi-source datasets into dynamic dashboards, optimized ETL pipelines, and scalable, high-availability models that aggressively drive strategic corporate decisions and protect revenue margins.
